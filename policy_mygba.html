myGBA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CreativeApp ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in myGBA (the "App"), a Game Boy, Game Boy Color, and Game Boy Advance emulator. It covers the App, not independent services or websites you choose to use.

1. Local Gameplay and Storage

The App imports and runs game files (ROMs) on your device. ROM contents, ROM hashes, battery saves, save states, library records, settings, and controller mappings are processed or stored locally. We do not operate a cloud ROM-hosting, save-sync, or backup service, and ordinary gameplay does not upload your ROMs or saves to us. Device or operating-system backups and file providers you choose may separately handle App data under their own settings and policies.

2. Online Artwork and Game Metadata

The App may automatically download game catalogs and cover artwork from Libretro repositories hosted on GitHub. ROM hashes are matched against downloaded catalogs on your device; ROM contents and hashes are not uploaded for matching. Artwork requests include a catalog game title in the requested URL, so the hosting provider can infer the requested game. GitHub and its delivery infrastructure receive normal connection information, including your IP address, request URL, request time, and HTTP headers. This feature is separate from local emulation.

3. Wi-Fi ROM Transfer

When you enable Wi-Fi transfer, the App starts a temporary HTTP server on your local network and may advertise it using Bonjour. A browser on the network can send a ROM filename and file contents using the session PIN shown in the App. These files are transferred to your device rather than a CreativeApp server. Local network addresses and service information are used to establish the connection.

HTTP transfer is not encrypted; a PIN does not provide transport encryption. Use a trusted private network, keep the PIN private, and close the transfer screen when finished. The network, browser, or device operator may be able to observe transfer information.

4. Support and Feedback

If you submit in-app feedback, the App sends your message, optional contact email, app bundle identifier, app version and build, operating-system version, and device type to a Google Apps Script endpoint used for support. If you email us, we also receive the message and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond, diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and improve the App. Do not include ROMs, saves, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information in feedback.

5. Purchases and Payment Data

Apple processes purchases and subscriptions through the App Store. The App uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information, including product and expiry or revocation details, to unlock features and restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Apple may separately make transaction or sales information available to developers under its own policies.

6. Permissions, Advertising, and Analytics

The App uses system file selection for files you choose to import and local-network access for Wi-Fi transfer. You can manage applicable permissions in your device settings; denying access may prevent the corresponding feature. The App does not require camera, microphone, or photo-library access for emulation.

The current App does not integrate third-party advertising, Firebase Remote Config, or an analytics SDK. Network providers may still process connection information when online features are used. If our data practices change, we will update this Policy and obtain consent where required.

7. Use, Disclosure, and International Processing

We use information for the features described above, support, security, fraud prevention, compliance, and protecting legal rights. Where applicable, the legal basis is performance of a contract, consent, legitimate interests in providing and protecting the App, or legal obligations.

Support and related information may be processed by service providers, disclosed where required by law or to protect rights and safety, or transferred to a successor in a merger, reorganization, or sale subject to applicable privacy obligations. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Google, GitHub, Apple, and their providers may process data outside your country; applicable transfer safeguards are used where legally required. We do not send locally stored ROMs or saves to these providers through ordinary gameplay.

8. Retention, Deletion, and Backups

Local game data remains until you delete it or the relevant App storage is removed. Deleting a library entry may remove its associated ROM and saves. Removing the App may remove local data depending on the platform. We cannot restore data for which we have no server-side copy. Copies in device backups, exported files, or third-party storage must be managed separately by you.

We retain support and other information we hold only as reasonably necessary for support, security, legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes. You can request deletion using the contact below; legally required or permitted retention may still apply.

9. Choices and Privacy Rights

You can choose whether to submit feedback or enable Wi-Fi transfer, control applicable system permissions, and remove local game data. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, obtain a portable copy of personal information we hold, withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may reasonably verify identity and apply exceptions permitted by law. Requests concerning information held independently by Apple, Google, or GitHub should also be directed to that provider.

10. Security and Children

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no device, storage method, or network transmission is completely secure. Protect your device, backups, accounts, and local network.

The App is not directed to children under 13 or a higher minimum age required by local law for independent consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information should contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Policies

Online services have their own privacy practices: GitHub Privacy Statement, Google Privacy Policy, and Apple Privacy Policy. We do not control their independent processing.

12. Changes and Contact

We may revise this Policy as features or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. A policy update does not itself replace consent required by law.

For privacy questions or requests, contact CreativeApp at creative.app.support@gmail.com.
