Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CreativeApp ("CreativeApp", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use ScannerApp (the "App"). It applies only to the App and not to third-party services that you choose to use.

1. Summary

The App is designed to create and manage documents on your device. Scanned pages, imported files, document names, annotations, OCR text, and signatures are stored locally on your device unless you deliberately export, share, save, or upload them through a destination you select. We do not operate a document-backup or document-hosting service through the App.

2. Information We Process

Content you choose to process

The App accesses camera images when you scan, photos or files when you choose to import them, and document content when you edit, convert, search, annotate, sign, or run OCR. These operations are performed on your device. We do not receive or remotely store that content merely because you use these features.

If you choose to export, share, print, save to Photos or Files, or upload content using a service or destination, that content is handled by the receiving app, provider, or service under its own terms and privacy policy. You control that decision and are responsible for ensuring that your content may be shared.

Support and feedback

If you contact us or submit feedback, we process the information you provide, such as your email address, message, attachments, and technical details needed to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App, and improve our services. Do not include sensitive, confidential, or unnecessary document content in feedback.

Purchase information

Apple processes App Store purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card information. We may process transaction, entitlement, product, and refund-related information supplied by Apple to provide paid features, restore purchases, prevent fraud, maintain records, and respond to Apple refund processes.

Advertising, configuration, and device information

The App uses Google Mobile Ads to display ads and Firebase Remote Config to configure and improve App behavior. These providers may process technical and online identifiers, such as device and app information, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, diagnostic information, ad-interaction information, and consent choices, subject to their own policies and your device or consent settings. The App may show a consent message where required before requesting ads. We do not provide your locally stored documents, OCR text, signatures, or scanned images to these providers for advertising or remote configuration.

3. Permissions

We request camera permission to scan paper documents; photo-library permission to import images into documents; and add-to-photo-library permission to save exported images. You can deny or revoke a permission in iOS Settings, but the relevant feature will be unavailable. The App does not request a permission unless you invoke a feature that needs it.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to operate and secure the App; provide scans, imports, exports, purchases, support, and feedback handling; serve and measure advertising where permitted; configure features; comply with legal obligations; enforce our rights; and detect, investigate, or prevent fraud, misuse, or technical problems. We process information only where a lawful basis applies, including performance of a contract, consent, legitimate interests, and legal obligations, as applicable.

5. Sharing and International Transfers

We may disclose information to providers that help operate advertising, remote configuration, payments, support, security, or legal compliance; to affiliates or a successor in a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale; when required by law or valid legal process; or to protect rights, safety, property, and security. Providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers. We do not sell or share your locally stored document content because we do not receive it through ordinary App use.

6. Retention and Deletion

Local documents remain on your device until you delete them, remove the App, reset the device, or otherwise remove the relevant storage. We cannot recover local documents that are deleted, lost, damaged, unavailable, or removed with the App. You are responsible for maintaining backups and for deleting exports or copies stored in other apps, Files, Photos, iCloud, or third-party services.

We retain support, feedback, purchase, and technical information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, including legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and enforcement needs. We may retain de-identified or aggregated information. To request deletion of personal information held by us, contact us at the address below; some information may be retained where law permits or requires it.

You may control device permissions through iOS Settings, control advertising preferences through Apple’s privacy and advertising settings, and withdraw advertising consent where the App makes that option available. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information we hold, and to complain to a supervisory authority. To exercise a right, email us from an address that lets us reasonably verify your request. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity and may decline or limit requests as permitted by law.

You are responsible for device security, passcodes, backups, access to exported documents, and the security settings of services you choose for sharing or storage.

The App is not directed to children under the age at which they can lawfully consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.
