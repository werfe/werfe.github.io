Sudoku

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CreativeApp ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Sudoku (the "App"), a logic puzzle game. It covers the App, not independent services or websites you choose to use.

1. Local Gameplay and Storage

Puzzles are generated on your device. Your current game, notes, statistics, Daily Challenge history, streaks, and settings are stored locally. We do not operate an account system or cloud save service, and ordinary gameplay does not upload your game progress to us. Device or operating-system backups you choose may separately handle App data under their own settings and policies.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays ads served by Google AdMob, including app-open ads, interstitial ads shown between games, and optional rewarded ads you may choose to watch for extra hints. To serve, measure, and limit the frequency of ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect device and usage information such as IP address, device model, operating-system version, app version, ad interactions, coarse location derived from IP address, and, only where you allow it, the device advertising identifier (IDFA).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy Options in the App. Where required, the App also shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the IDFA is not shared and you may still see non-personalized ads. The App works the same whether or not you allow tracking.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config. These services may process an app-instance identifier, device and operating-system information, app version, language, country derived from IP address, crash logs, and gameplay events such as starting or finishing a puzzle, the difficulty played, and hint usage. We use this information to understand how the App is used, fix crashes, and adjust settings such as ad frequency. We do not send your name, email address, or contacts to Firebase, and analytics are not used to identify you personally.

4. Purchases and Payment Data

The App offers an optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. Apple processes this purchase through the App Store. The App uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock the purchase and restore it on your devices. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may separately make transaction or sales information available to developers under its own policies.

5. Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the App may submit scores and achievements to Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles your Game Center profile and the visibility of your scores under its own settings and policies. We do not receive your Apple ID credentials.

6. Support and Feedback

If you email us, we receive your message, email address, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond, diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and improve the App. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

7. Use, Disclosure, and International Processing

We use information for the features described above, support, security, fraud prevention, compliance, and protecting legal rights. Where applicable, the legal basis is consent (for personalized advertising and tracking), performance of a contract, legitimate interests in providing, improving, and protecting the App, or legal obligations.

Information may be processed by the service providers named in this Policy, disclosed where required by law or to protect rights and safety, or transferred to a successor in a merger, reorganization, or sale subject to applicable privacy obligations. We do not sell personal information for money. Where personalized advertising counts as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, you can opt out by declining tracking and personalized ads as described above. Google, Apple, and their providers may process data outside your country; applicable transfer safeguards are used where legally required.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains until you erase it in the App or delete the App. We cannot restore data for which we have no server-side copy. Google retains advertising and Firebase data according to its own retention settings and policies; Firebase Analytics data associated with the App is retained for no longer than 14 months. You can request deletion of information we hold using the contact below; legally required or permitted retention may still apply.

9. Choices and Privacy Rights

You can change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy Options, allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, purchase Remove Ads, and erase local game data. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, obtain a portable copy of personal information we hold, withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may reasonably verify identity and apply exceptions permitted by law. Requests concerning information held independently by Apple or Google should also be directed to that provider.

10. Security and Children

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no device, storage method, or network transmission is completely secure.

The App is not directed to children under 13 or a higher minimum age required by local law for independent consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Policies

Google provides AdMob, the User Messaging Platform, and Firebase. Apple provides App Store purchases, App Tracking Transparency, and Game Center. GitHub hosts our online legal pages. These services have their own privacy practices: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Privacy and Security in Firebase, Apple Privacy Policy, and GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not control their independent processing.

12. Changes and Contact

We may revise this Policy as features or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.

For privacy questions or requests, contact CreativeApp at creative.app.support@gmail.com.
