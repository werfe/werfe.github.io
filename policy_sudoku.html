Sudoku

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CreativeApp ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Sudoku (the "App"), a logic puzzle game. It covers the App, not independent services or websites you choose to use.

1. Local Gameplay and Storage

Puzzles are generated on your device. Your current game, notes, statistics, Daily Challenge history, streaks, and settings are stored locally. We do not operate an account system or cloud save service. The App sends certain gameplay events to Firebase when analytics collection is enabled and submits scores and achievements to Game Center when you are signed in, as described below. Device or operating-system backups you choose may separately handle App data under their own settings and policies.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays ads through Google AdMob, including app-open ads, ads between games, and optional rewarded ads for extra hints. Google may process your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, device identifiers such as the identifier for vendor (IDFV), ads shown and interactions with them, and technical diagnostics such as crash logs and app performance measurements. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often ads appear, prevent fraud, and diagnose and improve its advertising services. Some of this information can be associated with your device.

The App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to determine whether a privacy message applies, present it where required, and manage your choices. UMP may process general location, interactions with the consent form, and technical performance information to provide this feature. You can review or change your choices under Settings > Privacy Options when that option is available.

Google Mobile Ads can send the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you grant permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. When authorized and permitted by your applicable ad choices, Google may use advertising identifiers to match activity across companies’ apps or websites for targeted advertising or ad measurement. If you do not authorize tracking, the App disables ad personalization through its ad request configuration and Google Mobile Ads does not send IDFA. The App also configures Apple’s SKAdNetwork so participating ad networks can measure ad-driven app installs using Apple’s attribution system. You may still see non-personalized ads, and Google may still process information for uses such as ad delivery, frequency limits, measurement, fraud prevention, and diagnostics.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Firebase)

When your consent choices allow analytics storage, Firebase Analytics uses an app-instance identifier and collects information such as app version, device and operating-system information, language, general location, and automatically recorded events such as first opens, sessions, and eligible in-app purchases. We also send gameplay events such as starting or completing a puzzle, winning or losing, and using a hint. These events can include the difficulty and elapsed gameplay time. We use this information to understand how the App is used and improve it. These events can be associated with your App installation. We do not send your name, email address, or contacts to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports, including stack traces, relevant application state, device and operating-system information, and related technical diagnostics. It uses a Crashlytics installation identifier to group reports and may include recent in-app events when Analytics is enabled. Firebase also uses technical performance and session information to monitor app quality. We use this information to diagnose failures and improve stability.

Firebase Remote Config uses a Firebase installation ID and information such as your device’s country and language settings, time zone, operating system, and App information to fetch and apply settings such as how often ads are shown. When Analytics data is available, Remote Config can also use applicable analytics properties to select settings. Crashlytics and Remote Config operate separately from the Analytics consent setting.

4. Purchases and Payment Data

The App offers an optional “Remove Ads” purchase. Apple processes the purchase and handles your payment information. The App checks StoreKit transaction and entitlement information on your device to unlock or restore the purchase. When Firebase Analytics collection is enabled, Firebase can automatically record that a purchase occurred, along with details such as the product identifier, value, currency, and quantity, for purchase analytics associated with your App installation. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may separately make transaction or sales information available to developers under its own policies.

5. Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the App may submit scores and achievements to Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles your Game Center profile and the visibility of your scores under its own settings and policies. We do not receive your Apple ID credentials.

6. Support and Feedback

If you email us, we receive your message, email address, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond, diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and improve the App. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

7. Use, Disclosure, and International Processing

We use information for the features described above, support, security, fraud prevention, compliance, and protecting legal rights. Where applicable, the legal basis is consent (for personalized advertising and tracking), performance of a contract, legitimate interests in providing, improving, and protecting the App, or legal obligations.

Information may be processed by the service providers named in this Policy, disclosed where required by law or to protect rights and safety, or transferred to a successor in a merger, reorganization, or sale subject to applicable privacy obligations. We do not sell personal information for money. Where personalized advertising counts as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, you can opt out by declining tracking and personalized ads as described above. Google, Apple, and their providers may process data outside your country; applicable transfer safeguards are used where legally required.

8. Retention and Deletion

You can erase the current saved board in Settings; the App records its abandonment in local history. Other local App data generally remains until you delete the App, subject to your device and backup settings. We cannot restore data for which we have no server-side copy. Google retains advertising and Firebase data according to its own retention settings and policies; Firebase Analytics data associated with the App is retained for no longer than 14 months. You can request deletion of information we hold using the contact below; legally required or permitted retention may still apply.

9. Choices and Privacy Rights

When available, you can change ad consent in Settings > Privacy Options, allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, purchase Remove Ads, and erase the current saved board in the App. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, obtain a portable copy of personal information we hold, withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may reasonably verify identity and apply exceptions permitted by law. Requests concerning information held independently by Apple or Google should also be directed to that provider.

10. Security and Children

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no device, storage method, or network transmission is completely secure.

The App is not directed to children under 13 or a higher minimum age required by local law for independent consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact us so we can take appropriate action.

These services have their own privacy practices: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Privacy and Security in Firebase, Apple Privacy Policy, and GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not control their independent processing.

12. Changes and Contact

We may revise this Policy as features or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.
